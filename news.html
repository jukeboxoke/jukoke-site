Get Ready: a guided start, and easier-to-read help text

October 10, 2026

Getting from install to your first song should not need a manual.

  • Get Ready (NICE-Start). A guided checklist that opens the first time you run Jukoke. Tell it what you have to sing with: song files, your own media, or nothing yet. It then checks your screen, song folders, catalog, online search and Wi-Fi as they are right now, and takes you straight to whatever is left. Skip anything you do not plan to use, and bring it back later. Find it any time under Setup, or on the Dashboard when something needs a look. On every plan.
  • Online search still needs a separate helper program that you choose to set up. It is provided as is, without warranty or support, and Get Ready can test the connection for you.
  • Offline song folders. If a drive is unplugged, a song from it now shows a clear error on the TV and in the Played list, then Jukoke returns to the idle screen and carries on.
  • Easier to read. Hints, instructions and other secondary text are brighter and larger on the host dashboard and on guests’ phones.
  • Song Catalog now shows how many songs it holds at a glance.

Announce, a managed Ask the MC list, and a clearer end of session

October 9, 2026

More tools for running a busy room.

  • Announce (Pro). Send one short message to every guest’s phone: Song submissions close in 10 minutes, Someone found a wallet. Choose Info, Good news or Urgent, whether guests must tap OK, and whether it also shows on the TV, as a banner across the top that never covers the lyrics.
  • Ask the MC list (Lite and Pro). Waiting and Handled tabs, search, and sorting by oldest, newest or guest. Each request shows the guest’s emoji and name so similar nicknames are easy to tell apart. Guests can take a message back before you answer it.
  • Start new session. When a session has run out for good there is nothing left to renew, so the button now says what it does, and shows progress while it works.
  • Your own background picture on guests’ phones is now part of Pro, alongside the TV background. Lite parties keep their title and colors, with a wave background that follows them.
  • Clearer joining. A guest picks a nickname and a 4-digit PIN. The last four digits of a phone number make an easy PIN, but any four digits work.

Easier to join, easier to ask: release 2026.10.08-001

October 8, 2026

This release is about getting guests singing faster.

  • Two-step join codes. Step 1: Join Wi-Fi and Step 2: Join Jukoke now sit side by side under Party → QR Code / Link, on every plan.
  • Ask the MC (Lite and Pro). Guests send you a private note, you add songs to their own queue and reply with a green, blue or red answer. Badges on Dashboard and Live Guests show what is waiting.
  • Ask MC is available on phones and on desktop browsers, and notes can use any language.
  • Roomier Now Playing controls so the stats card stays on screen when Hold is shown.
