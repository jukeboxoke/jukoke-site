Refunds

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Try before you buy

Jukoke is free to run, and every installation includes a free trial window for the paid plans, so you can see what you’re buying before you spend anything.

If something goes wrong

If a purchase didn’t deliver the tokens you paid for, or a paid session failed because of a fault in Jukoke, email support@jukeboxoke.com with the email address you used and a short description. We’ll fix it by crediting the tokens, or by refunding your payment when that is the right outcome.

Spent tokens

Tokens already spent on a session or add-on are not refundable once the session has started, except where there was a fault as described above.
