Common questions

Guests can’t open the join link

Make sure their phones are on the same WiFi network as the computer. If the address in the QR code looks wrong, correct it in Party → QR Code / Link. If a link won’t open from a messaging app, use Copy Hostname Link instead. On Windows that may need Apple’s Bonjour installed.

A guest can’t join, or can’t add songs

They may be waiting for your approval (Live Guests → Pending Review), the party may be at the guest limit for your plan, or they may be blocked. If they can join but not add songs, the queue may be full for your plan, or the session may be in its short grace period after the 6 hours are up.

The TV picture is on the wrong screen, or went blank

Open Setup → Display, tap Refresh and choose the screen you want. If the picture goes blank after the computer wakes from sleep, use Refresh Player Display in the same place.

A song failed to play

Jukoke skips to the next song automatically. Check the Played list for the reason. If the same song keeps failing, blacklist it.

Guests can’t find my songs when they search

Songs have to be approved in the catalog, the folder they live in must be added and active under Song Source → Song Library, and on Lite or Pro guests need Allow Song Catalog Search. The in-app guide walks through each step.

Does Jukoke come with songs?

No. You bring your own karaoke files. Online sources are optional and set up by you. You’re responsible for having the right to play the songs you use, and venues generally need the appropriate public-performance licences.

How do Play Tokens and sessions work?

Jukoke is free to run on its own. For a bigger party you start a 6-hour session from the Wallet (Party → Wallet) using Play Tokens. Nothing is charged until you confirm, and you see your balance before and after. Buy tokens with Buy More. If you bought a pack, your redeem code arrives by email; type it into the box under Have a code? and tap Redeem. Codes work once, on one installation, and need an internet connection when you redeem.

I bought tokens but they haven’t arrived

Check your email, including spam, for the code and redeem it in the Wallet. If nothing arrives within a few minutes, email support@jukeboxoke.com with the email address you used to buy.

I forgot the MC password I set

On the Jukoke computer, delete the hidden file data/.mc_auth.json and restart Jukoke. This returns the factory password from your install. Passwords are case-sensitive.

Windows shows a “Windows protected your PC” message when I install

Choose More info, then Run anyway. You can check the file first against the SHA-256 value on the download page.

How do I uninstall?

Use Windows Settings → Apps, or Ubuntu’s Software app. Your songs, history and settings stay in place unless you delete them.
