Good to know

  • Free is yours to keep: the MC and one guest, with guided setup and party preparation.
  • You see your balance before you start a session, and nothing is charged until you confirm.
  • Tokens have no cash value. See the refund policy and the terms.
  • Tips from your guests go straight to you. Jukoke takes no fee.

Prices and plan contents may change at any time. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay, and tokens you have already bought keep their value. Last updated October 2026.
