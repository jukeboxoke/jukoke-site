Pricing

Jukoke is free to run on its own. When you want a bigger party, start a session — a 6-hour block of a paid plan — using Play Tokens. One token is $1. No subscription, and unused tokens never expire.

Upgrading a running session

Move from Lite to Pro at any time and pay only the difference: 5 tokens.

See what each plan includes.

30-Day Pro Pass

$35 for 30 days of Pro. While your pass is active, every Lite or Pro session you start is free, as many as you like, each lasting 6 hours. Buy it in Buy More in the Wallet, up to 3 at a time.

  • A pass waits in your Wallet until you tap Activate, and it never expires while it waits.
  • Passes stack: activate another when one ends and the 30 days run back to back.
  • A pass session can’t be extended or upgraded with tokens, and you can’t start a token-paid session while a pass is active.

Free trial

Every installation includes a 30-day free trial of the paid plans. Claim it within 30 days of first running Jukoke; claiming needs an internet connection.

Good to know

  • You never pay twice: upgrading Lite to Pro costs only the difference.
  • Free is yours to keep: the MC and one guest, with guided setup and party preparation.
  • You see your balance before you start a session, and nothing is charged until you confirm.
  • Tokens have no cash value. See the refund policy and the terms.
  • Tips from your guests go straight to you. Jukoke takes no fee.

Prices and plan contents may change at any time. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay, and tokens you have already bought keep their value. Last updated October 2026.
