Easy for the host. Easy for every guest.

Most karaoke setups make someone do the work: the host juggling requests on paper, or guests queuing at a laptop. Jukoke gives each side exactly what they need and nothing more.

For the host

  • Get ready before anyone arrives. Prepare your songs, your guest list and the look of the night ahead of time (NICE-Prep).
  • Run the night from your phone. A fair queue that looks after itself (NICE-Q).
  • Step in when you need to. Reorder, hold a turn, approve or block a guest (NICE-GAME).

For your guests

  • Scan and you’re in. Come back next time and Jukoke remembers you, with your songs and keys (NICE-Join).
  • Find a song however you’ve got it. The host’s library, online, or a pasted link (NICE-Add).
  • Get a fair turn and sing your way. Control your own song, and practise the tricky bit first (NICE-Play, NICE-Try).

How a party works

  1. Start Jukoke

    Open it on the computer plugged into your TV and sound system. A QR code appears on the screen. Want the night perfect? Prepare it the day before.

  2. Guests scan and add songs

    Everyone joins on their own phone, picks a nickname, and searches for songs. Nothing to install, no account to make.

  3. Everyone gets a turn

    The queue rotates fairly, so one enthusiastic singer can’t hog the night. You can reorder, skip or step in whenever you need to.
