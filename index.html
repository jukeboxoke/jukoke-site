Zero install. Low friction.

Every extra step costs you a guest. Most karaoke apps ask everyone to download something and sign up before they can sing a note. Jukoke asks for a camera and a nickname.

The usual way

  • Find the app and download it
  • Make an account, confirm an email
  • Type the Wi-Fi password, then work out the party code
  • The host reads out requests, or guests crowd the laptop

The Jukoke way

  • Scan the Wi-Fi code to get on the network (optional)
  • Scan the Jukoke code to open the party in the phone’s browser
  • Pick a nickname and you’re in. No name, email or phone number is asked for
  • Come back next time and Jukoke remembers you

Works with the browser every phone already has — iPhone, Android, an old tablet someone found in a drawer.

Easy for the host. Easy for every guest.

Most karaoke setups make someone do the work: the host juggling requests on paper, or guests queuing at a laptop. Jukoke gives each side exactly what they need and nothing more.

For the host

  • Get ready before anyone arrives. Prepare your songs, your guest list and the look of the night ahead of time (NICE-Prep).
  • Run the night from your phone. A fair queue that looks after itself (NICE-Q).
  • Step in when you need to. Reorder, hold a turn, approve or block a guest (NICE-GAME).
  • Hear from shy guests, quietly. Guests can message you privately and you add the songs for them (Ask the MC).

For your guests

  • Scan and you’re in. Come back next time and Jukoke remembers you, with your songs and keys (NICE-Join).
  • Find a song however you’ve got it. The host’s library, online, or a pasted link (NICE-Add).
  • Not a tech person? Just ask. Send the MC a note and they’ll find and queue the song for you. Nobody else sees it.
  • Get a fair turn and sing your way. Control your own song, and practice the tricky bit first (NICE-Play, NICE-Try).

Ask the MC: a private line to the host

Not everyone wants to tap through a song search in front of friends. With Ask the MC, a guest types a short note — “Could you find ‘Dancing Queen’ for me?” — and goes back to enjoying the party. The host adds the songs to that guest’s own place in the line and replies with a quick green, blue or red note. It’s quiet, discreet and fair, and it works in any language your phone can type. Lite & Pro

How a party works

  1. Start Jukoke

    Open it on the computer plugged into your TV and sound system. The join codes appear on the screen. Want the night perfect? Prepare it the day before.

  2. Guests scan and add songs

    Everyone joins on their own phone: scan the Wi-Fi code, scan the Jukoke code, pick a nickname and search for songs. Nothing to install, no account to make.

  3. Everyone gets a turn

    The queue rotates fairly, so one enthusiastic singer can’t hog the night. You can reorder, skip or step in whenever you need to.
